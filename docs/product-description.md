# HAIgher Education — Product Description

## Problem Statement

**AI is the defining challenge facing universities worldwide — in both teaching and research.** From small colleges to global research universities, a race is on to come to grips with what AI means for the future of higher education.

**In teaching**, educators face urgent questions: How do we assess learning when students have AI at their fingertips? How do we use AI as a learning aid without undermining genuine understanding, or compromising academic integrity?

**In research**, the stakes are just as high: AI is reshaping how literature is reviewed, data is analyzed, and papers are written. Researchers who master these tools will move faster; those who don't risk falling behind.

**No institution has solved this yet.** The result is a worldwide scramble — universities everywhere experimenting, drafting policies, and searching for a way forward, often in isolation and often reactively.

## Vision

HAIgher Education helps universities move from reactive scrambling to a confident, coherent strategy for AI in teaching and research — turning a threat into an advantage for students, educators, and researchers alike.

## Target Users

- **Educators & instructors** — designing courses, assessments, and policies for an AI-enabled classroom.
- **Researchers** — integrating AI into literature review, analysis, and writing responsibly and effectively.
- **Students** — learning to use AI as a genuine aid to understanding rather than a shortcut around it.
- **University leadership & administrators** — setting institution-wide policy, guidance, and enablement.

## Value Proposition

- **Shared knowledge instead of isolated experiments** — capture and spread what works across departments and institutions.
- **Practical guidance, not just policy** — concrete patterns for assessment, integrity, and AI-assisted workflows.
- **Faster, responsible adoption** — help staff and students adopt AI tools without sacrificing rigor or trust.

## Key Themes

1. **Teaching** — assessment redesign, academic integrity, and AI as a learning aid.
2. **Research** — AI-assisted literature review, data analysis, and writing.
3. **Policy & governance** — frameworks for responsible, institution-wide adoption.
