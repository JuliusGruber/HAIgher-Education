# HAIgher Education — Product Description

## Problem Statement

**AI is the defining challenge facing universities worldwide — in both teaching and research.** From small colleges to global research universities, a race is on to come to grips with what AI means for the future of higher education.

**In teaching**, educators face urgent questions: How do we assess learning when students have AI at their fingertips? How do we use AI as a learning aid without undermining genuine understanding, or compromising academic integrity?

**In research**, the stakes are just as high: AI is reshaping how literature is reviewed, data is analyzed, and papers are written. Researchers who master these tools will move faster; those who don't risk falling behind.

**No institution has solved this yet.** The result is a worldwide scramble — universities everywhere experimenting, drafting policies, and searching for a way forward, often in isolation and often reactively.

## The Product

**HAIgher Education is an always-on intelligence engine for AI in higher education.** It continuously scans the internet — globally and across languages — for everything published about AI and universities, structures what it finds into an evolving knowledge graph, and turns that knowledge into a weekly newsletter, a weekly blog, and an explorable website. In a field moving too fast for anyone to follow manually, HAIgher Education watches everything, so its audience doesn't have to.

### How It Works

**1. Continuous collection.** The engine constantly scrapes every available channel and format: news articles, official university announcements, researcher and educator blogs, LinkedIn and X posts, policy documents, and videos on platforms like YouTube (which are transcribed). Non-English sources are translated, so coverage is genuinely global.

**2. A living knowledge graph.** Everything collected is extracted into a knowledge graph connecting topics, institutions, people, tools, policies, and events. The graph is always evolving — it is the structured, up-to-date memory of the entire field.

**3. A self-improving loop.** The knowledge graph feeds back into collection: as new topics, terms, and actors emerge in the graph, the scraper's search terms adapt automatically. Coverage follows the field as it evolves, without anyone having to redefine what to look for.

**4. Published outputs.** The knowledge flows out through:

- **A weekly newsletter** — the essential developments of the week, compiled from everything collected.
- **A weekly blog post** — a deeper narrative take on a current theme or trend.
- **The homepage** — the knowledge graph made public: an interactive visual explorer, a trend dashboard showing what's rising and falling, and always-current topic pages (e.g., per theme or institution) generated from the graph.

All newsletter and blog content is AI-drafted and human-reviewed before publishing.

### Who It's For

- **University leadership** — provosts, deans, and strategy officers who need the full picture to set policy.
- **Educators and researchers** — who want to stay current without monitoring dozens of channels.
- **Students and the interested public** — anyone following how AI is transforming universities.

### Why HAIgher Education Wins

- **Completeness.** It monitors every channel and format — not just the headlines. Nothing escapes it.
- **Structure, not just news.** The knowledge graph reveals connections and trends that no news feed can show.
- **Neutrality and rigor.** Vendor-neutral, source-grounded, citation-backed — trustworthy in a hype-heavy field.
- **Self-improving coverage.** Because the graph steers the scraping, coverage automatically tracks the field as it changes.

### Launch Approach

All channels launch together — newsletter, blog, and the graph-powered homepage form one integrated product from day one. Everything is free initially: the goal is to build reach and authority as *the* reference source for AI in higher education. Monetization comes later, once the audience is established.
