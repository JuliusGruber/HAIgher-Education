# HAIgher Education — Product Description

## The Problem

**No one can keep up with what's happening at the intersection of AI and universities.**

AI is reshaping higher education on every front at once — teaching and assessment, research workflows, academic integrity, institutional policy, funding, and student life. The developments that matter are scattered across news outlets, university announcements, researcher and educator blogs, LinkedIn and X, policy PDFs, and conference talks — in dozens of languages, published faster than anyone can read.

The result: **the people who most need to stay informed can't.** Provosts setting policy, educators redesigning courses, and researchers adopting new tools each end up monitoring a handful of channels, in one language, and hoping they haven't missed what matters. There is no single, trustworthy place that tracks the whole "AI and universities" complex and makes sense of it.

## The Product

**HAIgher Education is an always-on intelligence engine that keeps you informed about AI in higher education — so you don't have to watch it yourself.**

It continuously scans the internet, globally and across languages, for everything published about AI and universities; structures what it finds into an evolving knowledge graph; and turns that knowledge into a weekly newsletter, a weekly blog, and an explorable website. One source, watching everything, told plainly.

### How It Works

**1. Continuous collection.** The engine constantly gathers from every available channel and format: news articles, official university announcements, researcher and educator blogs, LinkedIn and X posts, policy documents, and videos on platforms like YouTube (transcribed). Non-English sources are translated, so coverage reaches beyond the English-speaking web.

**2. A living knowledge graph.** Everything collected is extracted into a knowledge graph connecting topics, institutions, people, tools, policies, and events — the structured, up-to-date memory of the field.

**3. A self-improving loop.** The graph feeds back into collection: as new topics, terms, and actors emerge, the engine's search terms adapt, so coverage tracks the field as it evolves instead of chasing a fixed list of keywords.

**4. Published outputs.** The knowledge flows out through:

- **A weekly newsletter** — the essential developments of the week, compiled from everything collected.
- **A weekly blog post** — a deeper narrative take on a current theme or trend.
- **The homepage** — the knowledge graph made public: an interactive visual explorer, a trend dashboard showing what's rising and falling, and always-current topic pages (per theme or institution) generated from the graph.

All newsletter and blog content is AI-drafted and human-reviewed before publishing.

### Who It's For

Anyone who needs to stay current on AI in higher education without making it a second job:

- **University leadership** — provosts, deans, and strategy officers who need the full picture to set policy.
- **Educators and researchers** — who want to stay current without monitoring dozens of channels.
- **Students and the interested public** — anyone following how AI is transforming universities.

### Why HAIgher Education Is Different

- **One place for the whole field.** It watches every channel, format, and language, so you don't have to stitch the picture together from a dozen feeds.
- **Structure, not just news.** The knowledge graph reveals connections and trends across institutions, people, and topics that no linear feed can show.
- **Neutral and grounded.** Vendor-neutral and source-linked, so every claim traces back to where it came from.
- **Coverage that keeps up.** Because the graph steers the collection, the scope tracks the field as it changes.

## Free — and Free to Stay

HAIgher Education is free, and it will stay free. Its purpose is to be *the* reference source for AI in higher education and to make staying informed effortless for everyone in the field — not to sell access to it. All channels launch together: newsletter, blog, and the graph-powered homepage form one integrated product from day one.
